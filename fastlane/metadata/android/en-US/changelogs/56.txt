Spotify on the phone: as soon as a track starts, Soloist queues the next one, so "next" and the end of a track no longer wait for it to load (the Spotify app shows the next track queued on "Xonos"). After a skip the audio starts sooner, Sendspin goes straight to the server on the phone, proot no longer stops PulseAudio on every request: less CPU. The log says when the music is heard. After updating, tap "Update Music Assistant on this phone".
